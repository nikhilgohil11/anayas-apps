# Solfège Melody

A music-learning game for kids (ages ~6–7). Pick a nursery song, watch a solfège pad light up, and press **Do–Re–Mi–Fa–Sol–La–Ti** to play the melody.

**[Play the game →]({{ site.baseurl }}/play/)**

## How to play

1. Choose a song from the menu.
2. The next pad lights up and pulses.
3. Tap or click that pad — the note plays and the next pad lights up.
4. Wrong pad? Nothing advances; try the glowing one again.
5. Finish the sequence to see “Great job!”

Timing is forgiving: the game waits for the correct press.

Touch or mouse on the on-screen pads — built for tablets and phones as well as desktops.
